# Target Audience

The primary target audience is adults who are interested in escapism and the exploration of fantastical worlds and consume fantasy and roleplaying stories and games. The marketing strategy should focus on creating an immersive experience that captures the imagination and transports the audience into the story.

Secondary target audience is adults who like concepts such as professional sports and organized crime and would be interested in spec fit or fantasy that uses those elements.

Tailoring our marketing strategies and content to appeal to these diverse customer profiles will help us effectively reach and engage our target audience interested in escapism and fantastical worlds.

**Fantasy Enthusiasts:** These are passionate individuals who deeply appreciate the world of fantasy and speculative fiction. They enjoy exploring immersive and engaging experiences in this genre, and are avid readers, gamers, collectors, and fans of fantasy-themed artwork and merchandise.

**Creative Community:** This segment consists of aspiring writers, artists, and creators who are looking for inspiration and a platform to showcase their talent. They appreciate the originality and creativity of our Property and are interested in collaborating and connecting with like-minded individuals.

**Escape Seekers:** These individuals are looking for an escape from reality and a chance to immerse themselves in a captivating fictional world. They are drawn to the sense of wonder and excitement that the Property offers, and value the opportunity to explore a rich and immersive realm of imagination.

## Profiles of Hypothetical Customers

### The Enchanted Dreamer
- Age: 18-30
- Interests: Loves reading fantasy novels, playing role-playing video games, and attending cosplay events.
- Personality: Imaginative, creative, and enjoys exploring new worlds and characters.
- Motivation: Seeks immersive experiences that transport them to otherworldly realms, seeking to escape from the mundane.

### The Tabletop Adventurer
- Age: 25-40
- Interests: Enjoys tabletop role-playing games like [[Dungeons & Dragons]], collects miniatures, and attends game nights.
- Personality: Strategic, collaborative, and enjoys storytelling and character development.
- Motivation: Looking for rich lore, compelling narratives, and fantastical settings to enhance their tabletop gaming experiences.

### The Digital Realm Explorer
- Age: 15-35
- Interests: Avid player of fantasy-themed video games, MMORPGs (Massively Multiplayer Online Role-Playing Games), and virtual reality experiences.
- Personality: Tech-savvy, adventurous, and seeks interactive and visually captivating worlds.
- Motivation: Craves digital fantasy experiences, looking for captivating stories and immersive environments.

### The Fantasy Enthusiast
- Age: 25-45
- Interests: Devours fantasy novels, enjoys attending Renaissance fairs, and collects fantasy-themed merchandise.
- Personality: Passionate, nostalgic, and values authentic world-building and character development.
- Motivation: Desires to explore new fantasy worlds, meet unique characters, and relive the magic of classic fantasy tales.

### The Escape Artist
- Age: 30-50
- Interests: Enjoys escape room adventures, participates in Live Action Role-Playing, and follows fantasy series.
- Personality: Adventurous, social, and seeks real-world interactions with fantasy elements.
- Motivation: Looks for opportunities to step into fantasy scenarios, solve mysteries, and immerse themselves in alternate realities.

### The World Builder
- Age: 20-35
- Interests: Creative writing, world-building, and designing their own role-playing campaigns.
- Personality: Innovative, detail-oriented, and enjoys crafting their own stories and settings.
- Motivation: Seeks inspiration and resources for their own creative projects, making them an ideal customer for lore-rich content.

### The Storytelling Enthusiast
- Age: 30-50
- Interests: Enjoys narrating stories to others, hosts storytelling events, and appreciates intricate plotlines.
- Personality: Expressive, community-oriented, and values compelling narratives.
- Motivation: Looking for captivating stories to share and discuss with others, fostering a sense of belonging in the fantasy storytelling community.
