Monster Sports Federation

The Monster Sports Federation (MSF) is official governing body of organized monster competition of the nation of Pluralis. Established to promote fair play, athletic excellence, and the spirit of competition among monsters of all kinds, the MSF organizes a variety of events ranging from traditional monster battles to innovative new sports that showcase the unique abilities of its participants.

The stated objectives of the Monster Sports Federation include:

  • Establishing and enforcing safety standards for monster training and competition.
  • Organizing seasonal championships and special events.
  • Licensing trainers and facilities.
  • Developing training programs and certification systems.
  • Promoting monster sports as mainstream entertainment.
  • Managing monster breeding and trading regulations.
