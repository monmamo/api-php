The mission of the Federation of Nations, as articulated in the Great Agreement and ratified by the nations of Erebis, Asis and Pluralis, is as follows:

  • Maintaining international peace and security.
  • Protecting the interests of minor states.
  • Developing friendly relations among the three major states and all minor states.
  • Promoting cooperation among the three major states and all minor states.
  • Serving as a center for harmonizing the actions of states in achieving those goals.
