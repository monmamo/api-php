Pluralis

The nation governing the western hemisphere per the terms of the Great Agreement.

So named because it is one land governing many different peoples.

History

Founded in the aftermath of the Five Centuries of War as part of the Great Agreement, Pluralis was created to provide a unified government for the diverse anthropes inhabiting the western hemisphere. The nation was established with the goal of promoting peace, cooperation, and mutual respect among its various peoples, and maintaining balance with the two other great nations Erebis and Asis.

Efforts to unify the peoples of the two continents begins in the 60th Century as the indigenous peoples face invasions and colonization from both Europe and Asia.
