Verona Waxwood

Verona Waxwood is a tall orc woman of the Vitquin race. She has pale green skin, shoulder-length black hair, and piercing yellow eyes. An athletic woman, she is known for her strength and agility, as well as her keen intellect.

As the daughter of Boris Waxwood, Verona is prctically born into the Monster Sports Federation. She becomes a monster trainer at an early age and is already competing when she becomes a teenager. Through her father and her own training, she sees the inner workings of the monster sports industry. And she doesn't always like what she sees. She emerges from childhood as the polar opposite of her father: While Boris cares more for business than monster training, Verona cares nothing for the business side.

At age 20, she enters the Monster Sports Federation as a competitive trainer. She rises to the Champion League. She uses her success as a monster trainer as a means to an end, to give her money and celebrity that she needs to fund into a real cause.

Meanwhile, Verona becomes involved in Viridis, The leading modal organization that focuses on Floros and Folios monsters. Eventually, she runs for president of the organization. By that time, Boris has ascended to commissioner. In her declaration for the office, she criticizes the Monster Sports Federation in public for the first time. She questions the ascent of Amalica Switch and alleges that she and her people are having undue influence over her father. She claims that being president of Viridis would "give her a solid out from the Monster Sports Federation."

A week before the Viridis vote takes place, sports reporter Alvin Mondagrain airs a report on Verona Waxwood. In the report, he criticizes her as a "spoiled child" who has to get her way, and Boris Waxwood as a weak, ineffective executive who lets his daughter monopolize his attention. Verona uses it to go on the offensive, telling her faithful it reflects "bad faith" in the industry.

She wins by a substantial margin, but the election leaves a rift between father and daughter that never fully heals.
