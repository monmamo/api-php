Personality & Psychology

  • Moral Alignment: True Neutral
  • Enneagram: Type Three – The Achiever
  • Myers-Briggs: ESTJ

    • Order-loving
    • Career-focused
    • Strategic
    • Image-conscious
  • Worldview:
    • Sees good, evil, law, and chaos as prejudices and dangerous extremes

Assets

  • Strong organizational instincts
  • Loyal when interests align
  • Thrives in ordered systems
  • Effective planner and administrator

Flaws

  • Career obsession
  • Moral indifference
  • Avoids accountability
  • Emotionally distant
  • Inflexible
  • Easily manipulated by ambitious subordinates

Cares

  • He cares about his career.
    • He plans his career and focuses on his goals.
    • Every project he undertakes and every contact he makes is done with the knowledge of how it will further his career.
    • He doesn't waste time or effort.
  • He enjoys being the calm and centered person in the room to settle arguments and bring about order and peace.
  • He enjoys strategic planning and being part of a team.
  • Competition is fun to him. He respects others who are after the same promotion.
    • This helps blind him to Switch's evil. He respects and applauds her ambition until it is too late for him.

Fears

  • He fears losing his career. He loves what he does for a living; it's his identity and whole reason for being.
    • This will make retiring difficult for him; even more so as Switch forces it on him before he is ready.
    • Switch uses this trait to break him.
  • He doesn't understand his emotions. Intimacy is foreign to him.
    • His emotional distance and failure to comprehend intimacy undermines his relationships with his wife and Verona Waxwood.
  • Chaos is his enemy; he isn't equipped to handle anything spontaneous or random.
    • He must know where things fit and why.
    • He is always thinking logically and striving for order in his live as he strives for it in his work.
    • This makes him the weak link in Operation Virtue.
  • He doesn't handle rejection well.

Motivators

  • Esteem and Self-Respect: He wants to be recognized for his efforts, but doesn't seek to stand apart from the team.
  • Competition: Competition can goad him into trying new things. Any chance to use his mental skills always grabs his attention.
  • Success: He will do anything to move up the corporate ladder.

Background & History

  • Family:

Profession & Role in the World

  • Primary Occupation: Executive administrator
  • Title: Commissioner of the Monster Sports Federation
  • Tenure: From age 47 to 75
  • Leadership Style:
    • Calm, managerial, non-confrontational
    • Delegates aggressively

Sports & Institutional Impact

  • Monster Training Experience: None
  • Primary Expertise: Business, administration, strategic planning
  • Core Mission as Commissioner:
    • Increase Federation revenue.
    • Increase social stature and legitimacy of monster sports.
  • Results:
    • Revenue increased by an order of magnitude
    • Monster training became a viable standalone business

He proved that one need not understand monsters to reshape monster sports.

Key Actions

  • Co-created and instituted the Monster Trainer Development and Certification Program.
    • Professionalized monster training as an industry
  • Allowed expansion of the Monster Sports Federation's influence through lobbying and regulatory engagement.
    • Approves hiring of Dewitt Flint.
    • Allows creation of full-time lobbying apparatus.
  • Perception:
    • Views regulation as a natural extension of Federation interests
    • Sees nothing alarming in expanding political influence

His passivity allows the Federation to become a regulatory power without scrutiny.

Corruption & Ethical Failure

  • View of Corruption:

  • Response to Ethical Complaints:
    • Indifference if violations remain invisible to the public
  • Executive Culture:
    • Encourages generous bonuses
    • Rewards results over integrity
  • Greatest Failure:
    • Accepts Amalica Switch's narrative regarding Ben Livingwater
    • Allows scapegoating to preserve institutional image
    • Publicly praises Switch for "cleaning house"

Waxwood does not commit evil—he legitimizes it through silence.

His attempt at moral action comes too late—and costs him his life.
