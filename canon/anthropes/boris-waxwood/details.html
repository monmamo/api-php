    • Cares

  • He cares about his career.
  • He plans his career and focuses on his goals.
  • Every project he undertakes and every contact he makes is done with the knowledge of how it will further his career.
  • He doesn't waste time or effort.
  • He enjoys being the calm and centered person in the room to settle arguments and bring about order and peace.
  • He enjoys strategic planning and being part of a team.
  • Competition is fun to him. He respects others who are after the same promotion.
  • This helps blind him to Switch's evil. He respects and applauds her ambition until it is too late for him.

    • Fears

  • He fears losing his career. He loves what he does for a living; it's his identity and whole reason for being.
  • This will make retiring difficult for him; even more so as Switch forces it on him before he is ready.
  • Switch uses this trait to break him.
  • He doesn't understand his emotions. Intimacy is foreign to him.
  • His emotional distance and failure to comprehend intimacy undermines his relationships with his wife and Verona Waxwood.
  • Chaos is his enemy; he isn't equipped to handle anything spontaneous or random.
  • He must know where things fit and why.
  • He is always thinking logically and striving for order in his live as he strives for it in his work.
  • This makes him the weak link in Operation Virtue.
  • He doesn't handle rejection well.

    • Motivators

  • Esteem and Self-Respect: He wants to be recognized for his efforts, but doesn't seek to stand apart from the team.
  • Competition: Competition can goad him into trying new things. Any chance to use his mental skills always grabs his attention.
  • Success: He will do anything to move up the corporate ladder.
