1. Anthropes

Boris Waxwood

As commissioner of the Monster Sports Federation, Boris Waxwood vowed to increase both the revenue of the Federation and its stature and presence in society. And he succeeded at both. The Federation's revenue increased by an order of magnitude, as did the business of monster training. But the success came at a high moral price. Boris ignored the corruption festering in the corporate hierarchy beneath him. He said little about the expulsion of Ben Livingwater, letting the league run with Amalica Switch's narrative that the league set an example and sufficiently cleaned itself.
