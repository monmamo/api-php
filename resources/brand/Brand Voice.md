## Personality: Encouraging

We want to sound like a knowledgeable peer who motivates creators to experiment, share boldly, and take part in shaping the world of __Monsters Masters & Mobsters__.

Examples:
- Celebrate effort, not just polished results: “This rough concept already has something electric in it—keep going!”
- Invite participation in open-ended ways: “What part of the world would __you__ build onto next?”
- Use language that empowers creators: “Your perspective brings something no one else can.”

## Tone: Bold

We aim to speak with confident clarity, unafraid to explore big ideas, tackle heavy themes, or challenge genre conventions.

Examples:
- Frame creative freedom as a strength: “This world doesn’t pull punches—and neither should you.”
- Be direct about the stakes or implications: “Magic here isn’t whimsical; it’s political, personal, and dangerous.”
- Highlight the brand’s distinctiveness: “This isn’t just another fantasy setting—it’s a speculative mirror of our world.”

## Energy: Chill and Grounded

We keep the vibe low-pressure and thoughtful—passionate without being performative, honest without being harsh.

Examples:
- Reassure creators that they’re not racing anyone: “Take your time. Great worldbuilding comes in layers.”
- Use measured praise: “There’s something solid here—let’s build on it.”
- Avoid hype for its own sake: “Not everything needs to be epic. Sometimes a street corner can hold an entire story.”

## Vocabulary: Precise but Accessible

We use clear, specific language that respects the intelligence of our audience without drowning them in jargon or academic tone.

Examples:
- Use world-specific terms only when they’re grounded in context: “A Handler isn’t just a trainer—they’re a tactician, a caretaker, a gambler.”
- Favor strong nouns and verbs over fluffy adjectives: “Fixers broker monster fights. Masters make monsters matter.”
- Write in sentence structures that are clear but varied: “Some stories start with a murder. Others start with a deal gone wrong and a beast in the basement.”
